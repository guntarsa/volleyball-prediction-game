Scoring System
Match Predictions:
  • 6 points: Perfect prediction (exact sets: 3-0, 3-1, 3-2)
  • 4 points: Correct winner, off by 1 set
  • 2 points: Correct winner only
  • 3 points: Predicted 5 sets, wrong winner (5-set game only)
  • 1 point: Correct total sets, wrong winner (non-5-set game)
Tournament Predictions:
  • 30 points: Tournament winner
  • 15 points: Predicting any medalist
  • +5 points: Exact position (2nd or 3rd place)
